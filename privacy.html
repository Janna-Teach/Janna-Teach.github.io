NanoGC privacy policy

NanoGC is a personal assistant run by its owner on her own computer, for her own Google account only.

  • Google data (Gmail, Drive, Calendar, Docs, Sheets, Slides, Forms, Tasks, Classroom, Contacts, Chat) is accessed only to carry out requests made by the owner.
  • Data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • OAuth tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
